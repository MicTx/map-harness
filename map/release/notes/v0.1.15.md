## What's new

**New**
- **Continuous map control**: let the agent pan, zoom, and inspect the live map within per-turn action, observation, and time limits while your manual takeover always wins.
- **Fixed historical basemaps**: select a verified Esri Wayback release without silent fallback to the latest imagery.
- **Visual map proposals**: review model-proposed sketches or targets from a captured map before they become annotations.
- **Two-image change review**: compare saved observations from fixed historical releases and confirm bounded appeared, disappeared, modified, or uncertain candidates.

**Fixed**
- **Durable map sessions**: map interactions, accepted observations, and visual decisions now survive restart and replay without recapturing images.
